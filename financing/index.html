PM Surya Ghar supports eligible residential grid-connected rooftop solar. For individual households in Maharashtra, the standard central assistance is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the next kW, capped at ₹78,000. Commercial properties are not covered by this household subsidy.

How to apply

  1. Register on the official national portal using your electricity consumer details and select your DISCOM.
  2. Submit the rooftop application and follow the portal’s feasibility and approval requirements before proceeding.
  3. Select a registered vendor and confirm scheme-compliant equipment, roof permissions and installation requirements.
  4. Complete installation, metering and DISCOM inspection / commissioning as instructed by the portal.
  5. Submit the required bank and claim details on the official portal. The authorities verify eligibility and determine the sanctioned payment.

Keep your electricity bill, roof permission, installation documents and bank details ready. Submit identity and banking documents only through official channels. Previous subsidies, extensions, housing societies, other states and special-category rates require separate checks. State top-ups are excluded here.
