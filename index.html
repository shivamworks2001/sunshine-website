End-to-end solar EPC

How We Deliver Your Solar Project

Understand the journey from enquiry to handover. Your quotation should confirm the scope and responsibilities for the specific installation.

  1. 01
    Initial Consultation

    Share electricity bills, usage patterns and your goals: bill reduction, independent supply or a specialist solar application.

  2. 02
    Site Assessment

    Evaluate space, shading, access, structural suitability and the existing electrical connection.

  3. 03
    Engineering & Design

    Develop the layout, component sizing and electrical arrangement around the assessed site and demand.

  4. 04
    Proposal & Planning

    Review the technical scope, commercial offer, exclusions, responsibilities and proposed schedule before approval.

  5. 05
    Installation & Execution

    Coordinate delivery and site access, then install the mounting, modules and electrical equipment with appropriate safety controls.

  6. 06
    Testing & Commissioning

    Check the installation and document test results. Grid-connected activation remains subject to the applicable utility process.

  7. 07
    After-Sales Support

    Explain operation, hand over documents and agree maintenance, service contacts and warranty support arrangements.
