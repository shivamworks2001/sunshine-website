01

Grid-connected rooftop solar plants

Grid-connected photovoltaic systems generate electricity for an on-site load and connect to the utility grid through solar panels, inverters, power-conditioning equipment and grid-connection hardware.

They can be considered for residential, commercial and larger rooftop applications. A site assessment is needed to establish the right design, connection approach and expected performance.

Request a rooftop assessment →
02

Off-grid solar power plants

Off-grid solar systems are designed for sites that need an independent source of electricity. System design considers the connected load, solar resource, energy storage and the operating needs of the site.

They can help reduce reliance on diesel-based supply where grid access is limited or unavailable. Final configuration should always follow a site survey and load assessment.

Plan an off-grid system →
03

Solar street lights

Solar street lights use a solar panel, rechargeable battery and light fitting to provide standalone illumination after daylight hours. They are suited to areas where grid connection is difficult or operating costs need to be reduced.

Location, lighting requirement and operating hours should be assessed before selecting a pole, panel and battery configuration.

Discuss solar lighting →
04

Solar water heaters

Solar water heating converts sunlight into heat through a solar thermal collector. Different configurations can support residential and selected industrial hot-water applications.

Sunshine Engineering can help identify a solution based on hot-water demand, available space and site conditions.

Explore water heating →
05

Solar water pumps

Solar-powered pumps operate using electricity generated by photovoltaic panels. They are particularly relevant where grid power is unavailable or where a lower-operating-cost water-pumping option is needed.

Pump selection depends on water source, head, flow requirement, seasonal demand and available solar area.
